Vet before you buy

A paid due-diligence service, so you know what you are really buying.

  1. Cash flow review. We examine bank statements, books and sales records to test the income the seller claims.
  2. KRA compliance check. We confirm tax registration, returns filed, and any outstanding liabilities.
  3. Licences and permits. We verify business registration, county licences and sector-specific permits are valid and current.
  4. Vetting report. You receive a written summary of findings and red flags before deciding.

Vetting is charged at a fee, agreed with you before any work starts. Contact us for a quote.

Terms of vetting

We provide an opinion after careful review of records, in joint consultation with our auditor. Although we exercise due diligence, Third Wave Solutions is not responsible for acts of omission or misrepresentation by clients, sellers or third parties.
